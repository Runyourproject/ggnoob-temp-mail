Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GGNoob Temp Mail (“we”, “the Service”) at {{ domain }} handles information when you use our free temporary email website.

1. What we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account, provide a real name, phone number, or permanent email address to use the temporary inbox feature. We do not sell personal data.

2. Information that may be processed

  • Email content: Messages sent to addresses on {{ domain }} are processed so they can be displayed in the web inbox. Mailboxes are not private; anyone who knows the address can read the messages.
  • Technical logs: Like most websites, our servers may log IP addresses, browser type, request times, and similar technical data for security, rate-limiting, and troubleshooting. These logs are retained only as long as needed for those purposes.
  • Cookies / local storage: We may use essential cookies or local storage for basic site function. We do not use them to build advertising profiles of individuals beyond what third-party ad networks may do.

3. Third-party services

The Service relies on external infrastructure to receive and store temporary messages (mail API provider). When you view an inbox, requests are made to that provider on your behalf.

If we display advertising (for example Google AdSense), the ad network may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads and measure performance. You can review Google’s policies and opt-out options at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We may also use privacy-friendly analytics (or none at all). Any analytics provider will be limited to aggregated, non-identifying metrics where possible.

4. Data retention

Temporary emails and their contents are short-lived by design and may be deleted automatically after a limited period (often measured in days). Server logs are rotated and not kept indefinitely. We do not offer permanent storage or account history.

5. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the website. However, because temporary addresses are public (no login required), you should never send sensitive personal data to a disposable inbox. Use this tool only for low-risk, short-term needs.

7. Your choices

You can stop using the Service at any time. Because there is no account, there is no profile to delete. Simply close the browser. If you believe messages related to you were sent to an address on this domain and you want them removed, contact us with sufficient detail so we can investigate within technical limits.

8. International users

The Service may be hosted or processed in different regions. By using it, you understand that information may be transferred to and processed where our servers or providers operate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The “Last updated” date will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the Contact page.
