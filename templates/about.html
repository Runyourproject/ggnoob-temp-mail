About GGNoob Temp Mail

GGNoob Temp Mail is a straightforward free temporary email service running on the domain {{ domain }}. We built it for people who need a disposable address quickly—without creating yet another account or handing over personal details.

Why temporary email?

Every day, websites ask for an email address. Some are legitimate; many will add you to marketing lists, sell or leak your data, or simply clutter your primary inbox for years. A temporary address lets you complete a signup, grab a verification code, or try a service, then walk away without long-term consequences for your real mailbox.

What we offer

  • Instant addresses on @{{ domain }} — random or custom
  • Live web inbox that refreshes automatically
  • No registration, no password, no personal profile
  • Clear Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
  • Mobile-friendly design that works on phones and desktops

Our approach

We keep the interface simple and the copy honest. The site is designed to be useful first, with advertising only as a way to support hosting. We aim to meet the quality and policy expectations of major ad networks so the service can remain free for everyone.

Temporary email is not a replacement for a real, private mailbox when you need security and long-term access. Use it for the jobs it is good at: short-lived verification, testing, and spam avoidance.
