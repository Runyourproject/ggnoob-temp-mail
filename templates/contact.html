How to reach us

For the time being, please use a temporary address of your choice on this site (or your own email) and send a message to the operator via the domain’s administrative contact if published in WHOIS, or open an issue if a public repository is linked later.

Abuse reports: Include the full temporary address involved, approximate time of the activity, and a short description. We will review reports within our technical ability on a public disposable-mail system.

Business / AdSense / partnerships: Use the same channel and mark the subject clearly.

Before you write

  • Message delivery depends on the sending service and our upstream mail provider; we cannot force a third party to accept disposable domains.
  • We cannot recover deleted temporary messages or guarantee long-term storage.
